# Flip Search website

The public pages for Flip Search, hosted free with GitHub Pages at
https://flipsearch.github.io/

- `index.html`: home page (the App Store **Marketing URL**)
- `support.html`: help, FAQ and the feedback form (the App Store **Support URL**)
- `thanks.html`: shown after someone sends the feedback form
- `privacy.html`: privacy policy (the App Store **Privacy Policy URL**)
- `app-ads.txt`: tells ad buyers which AdMob account may sell ads in the app
- `style.css`: the look of every page
